Open Data

Explore and download free Open Data from the City of Cornwall. Open Data is structured, machine-readable information that can be freely shared and used. You can explore datasets used daily by many City departments. As new datasets are released, we add them in here. We share Open Data for:

  • Better customer service
  • Stronger community engagement and collaboration
  • Faster map updates for new subdivisions (e.g., for delivery companies)
  • Better access to water, sewer, and road network data for investors and developers

Request specific information

Please make a data request online for specific information. Make sure to provide:

  • Short description: Enter a short one or two word description of the data requested. This field will be publicly visible. Please do not enter any identifiable personal information.
  • Detailed request description: This field will be publicly visible. Please do not enter any identifiable personal information.
  • Your name and contact email: For internal use only and will not be shared publicly.

Request additional information

By downloading or accessing this data, you are agreeing to the City of Cornwall's Open Government Licence.

Cornwall Transit Routes and Live Data

Access Cornwall Transit's GTFS (General Transit Feed Specification) information below. GTFS is a standardized, open data format for public transportation schedules, stops, and geographic paths. GTFS Real-time is an extension providing live, dynamic updates like vehicle positions and arrival delays.This data is hosted live by Metrolinx and currently accessed by TransitApp, Google Maps, Apple Maps, etc.
