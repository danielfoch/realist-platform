# Kawartha Lakes source audit

Audited October 3, 2026 Toronto time. The City and its [GeoHub](https://geohub-kawartha.hub.arcgis.com/) offer [Open Data Kawartha](https://open-data-kawartha.hub.arcgis.com/), whose native DCAT catalogue has 22 entries. These are catalogue entries, not property records or integrated feeds. City/community identities include Lindsay, Fenelon Falls, Bobcaygeon and Omemee with Ontario explicit; the broad Kawarthas region is not treated as the City.

## Reuse and independent provincial sources

The complete City-offered one-page [data terms](https://kawartha.maps.arcgis.com/sharing/rest/content/items/8166769837ab488785217db71b5c39b9/data) were read and visually reviewed after an ordinary publisher redirect. They contain reference-only disclaimers and site-use conditions, without an explicit compatible commercial merged-data redistribution grant for the selected municipal layers. Selected address, zoning, official-plan and heritage items have blank or reference-disclaimer grants. This is an unresolved reuse finding, not a categorical commercial-use ban.

The parcel/ARN item `6c54386e75424b78a4be4ec9ecae8677` separately prohibits sharing outside the City except a contractor under contract with a signed sharing agreement. No City feature/count/geometry queries or registry extraction/integration were performed. General public ARU guidance HTML contains an offered register; its presence is acknowledged, without extracting, counting or integrating the registry. No map agreement was accepted.

Two City-map items have specific OGL Ontario links and require separate treatment:

- The geographic lot item `14f52ed9426a4caba56a9bec4a71cdc1` describes a February 2023 provincial download, reprojection and clipping. Richer [original provincial lot/township feeds](./ONTARIO-FABRIC.md) are integrated independently; the City copy is not queried or counted.
- The Regulated Area item `fec5b12ee0064c24a83041cb8afa1559` has original metadata class `OHNWBDY`, identifying Ontario Hydro Network waterbody mapping for KRCA. Its title and OGL grant do not establish current conservation-regulation limits, floodplain or authority jurisdiction. No property screen was performed from that City item.

The separate [Kawarthas Naturally Connected original Ontario offer](https://data.ontario.ca/en/dataset/kawarthas-naturally-connected) is OGL licensed. Its 739-MB preferred-scenario archive reflects 2012 inputs and is not planned for maintenance. Original catalogue/item/metadata and ZIP central-directory inventory were audited without downloading/executing archive programs. GIS conversion/integration remains pending. It is a historical natural-heritage candidate, not current wetlands/flood/conservation regulation; no property screen or source-record count was performed.

## Realtor follow-ups

The API returns these as source-guidance gaps and document requests, with separate provincial evidence preserved:

- [Current planning initiatives](https://www.kawarthalakes.ca/business-development/planning-and-development/current-planning-initiatives/) reports rural consolidation adopted May 21, 2024, a July 16, 2026 whole-appeal decision, an outstanding review request and a site-specific appeal. It says former-township by-laws remain operative with portions settled January 2026 applying. The ruling itself was not reviewed. Obtain the current applicable former-township/settlement regime, written exceptions/holds, amendments and appeal/review outcomes. The zoning page names 19 by-laws while initiatives names 18; these differing statements are not reconciled into one property regime.
- [Official Plan and secondary plans](https://www.kawarthalakes.ca/business-development/planning-and-development/official-plan-and-secondary-plans/) offers a March 2025 office consolidation, separate schedules, four urban secondary plans and Oak Ridges Moraine material. Full PDFs, later amendments and current property applicability remain unverified. Obtain operative instruments and complete planning/Committee decisions, cleared conditions, approved plans and registered site-plan agreements.
- [Building and septic record searches](https://www.kawarthalakes.ca/property-environment/building-septic-and-renovations/building-and-septic-search-of-records/) are separate applications/fees requiring owner authorization when someone else requests them. Retention and pre-amalgamation gaps can affect building records; City building records do not retain legal surveys. A record search is separate from legal compliance/zoning confirmation. Obtain permit, final inspection, occupancy, approved septic design and servicing evidence. No application, payment or authorized record search has been made.
- [Additional-unit guidance](https://www.kawarthalakes.ca/property-environment/building-septic-and-renovations/additional-residential-units/) offers a public register and requires separate registration evidence for each ARU. Registration, inspections/occupancy and septic/servicing review are distinct. Rural lot/road/servicing, natural-area, agricultural-distance and ORM provisions need current property-specific confirmation; register absence would not establish illegality.
- [Heritage guidance](https://www.kawarthalakes.ca/business-development/planning-and-development/heritage-planning-and-permits/heritage-designation/) says the register is being updated and names Downtown Lindsay and Oak Street districts. Obtain current Part IV/Part V/listed status and complete designation/amending instruments from the Clerk.
- For waterfront/rural properties, obtain current survey/title, shoreline or road-allowance closure/conveyance, legal/year-round access and municipal road-assumption evidence. Confirm relevant current parcel-wide regulation and proposed-work requirements with the original authority. Original lot labels, raw road-allowance flags and waterbody mapping do not establish these facts.

Municipal core completion remains unproven. The roadmap marks Kawartha Lakes `audited_reuse_gap` with `complete=false`; the province-wide reference feeds are a separate baseline, not complete City property coverage.
