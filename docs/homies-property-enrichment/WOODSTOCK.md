# Woodstock and Oxford County source audit

Audited October 3, 2026. Woodstock gains a dated source-guidance audit with
explicit gaps, follow-up questions and document requests in the API, brief,
report, coverage, roadmap and hosted skill. No City/County property feed is
integrated or counted. Other Oxford municipalities remain queued for their own
audits. The independently licensed original Ontario lidar index is integrated
separately; see [ONTARIO-LIDAR.md](ONTARIO-LIDAR.md).

[City GIS guidance](https://www.cityofwoodstock.ca/business-development/maps-and-gis-information/)
says it has no public City GIS portal and offers County GLIMR. No map agreement
was accepted. The [County mapping page](https://www.oxfordcounty.ca/services-for-you/mapping/)
offers a separate [open-data catalogue](https://public-oxfordcounty.opendata.arcgis.com/).
The exact site `1b8a5ca1bd3d41e0a9a5a332784e31b2` supplies nine catalogue groups
and 22 catalogue entries. That entry count is neither property rows nor feeds.

Most item grants are blank; parcel-line and footprint notices are reference
disclaimers. The site's definition of the open-data idea does not establish an
operative compatible commercial merged-data grant for these selected feeds.
This is an unresolved grant, not a categorical ban on all open data.
The full [contractor data-request agreement](https://www.oxfordcounty.ca/services-for-you/mapping/data-requests/)
separately limits contractor data to internal unmodified copies and requires
permission for commercial products, prohibiting external posting. The full
[main website terms](https://www.oxfordcounty.ca/general/terms-of-use) separately
restrict duplication. Do not automatically apply either scope to independently
licensed catalogue data. No County feature/count/geometry requests were made.

[County Woodstock planning](https://www.oxfordcounty.ca/services-for-you/planning-and-development/development/woodstock/)
offers By-law 8626-10 text consolidated September 30, 2025, separate
awaiting-consolidation amendments and Q2 2026 mapping. Verify current written
exceptions/holds, temporary-use/MZO/parking schedules, later amendments, appeals
and plan applicability. Application notices/staff reports and draft site-plan
agreements are not final decisions, cleared conditions or registered instruments.
The City's general 8711-11 site-plan-control guidance needs current applicability
and exemption confirmation, rather than turning a process statement into law.

[Routine disclosure guidance](https://www.cityofwoodstock.ca/your-government/freedom-of-information/)
offers owner/authorized-agent plans/drawings and directs permit/application,
inspection-report and Building Code order access to a separate Legal Compliance
Letter. Older/off-site files can have gaps. No request was submitted or paid.
**Survey guidance conflicts:** the permit page says no survey copies are provided,
while routine disclosure offers some surveys subject to availability and warns
they can be outdated. Preserve the conflict, confirm release eligibility with the
City and obtain a current Ontario Land Surveyor/title review.

[Building reports](https://www.cityofwoodstock.ca/your-government/plans-reports-and-studies/building-reports/)
offer annual 2020–2025 and January–August 2026 monthly publications. Their PDF
contents, property rows and reusable publication rights remain unintegrated.
The current City privacy page has a heading/contact block without an explicit
publication grant; no broad City commercial prohibition is inferred. Report
offers/activity do not establish complete property history or passed inspections.

[ARU guidance](https://www.cityofwoodstock.ca/business-development/building-and-planning/additional-residential-units/)
describes conditional eligibility in R1/R2/R3/C3/HD with staff confirmation and a
building permit. Verify each advertised unit's approvals, final inspection,
occupancy and actual servicing; eligibility/incentives are not legality evidence.
The current full heritage register is unintegrated. The April 2026 Park Row
designation-passage notice is separate from final appeal/current status, and an
HD zone is not itself Part V heritage designation. Historical plans/magazine
counts are not a current statutory register. Original-authority regulation,
floodplain and source-protection rights/applicability remain unaudited; GLIMR
constraints and lidar package labels do not provide clearance or safety.
