# Owen Sound property research

Audited October 4, 2026. The report combines independently licensed County and
provincial references with explicit City research gaps and seller/authority
document requests. No City records, counts, geometry, register extraction,
property request, inspection or payment were obtained.

## Current planning transition

The City's [ZBA57 passing notice](https://media-001-ca.cdn.govstack.com/owensound-005-ca/media/3ctbyugv/notice-of-passing-zba-no-57.pdf)
and [OPA14 adoption notice](https://media-001-ca.cdn.govstack.com/owensound-005-ca/media/phjmhvo1/notice-of-adoption-opa-no-14.pdf)
are dated April 16, 2026 and describe April 13 passage/adoption of 2026-030 and
2026-029. ZBA57 gives a May 6 appeal deadline and expressly depends on OPA14
coming into effect. OPA14 requires County approval and may take effect in parts.
Current County decisions, appeals, operative text and schedules remain unverified.
The City's zoning/PIR pages still cite 2010-078. Passage, a final-file label and
an elapsed appeal deadline do not establish current permission.

Retain the source conflicts: the adoption notice says November 24, 2025 meetings,
while its appended adopted bylaw says November 24, 2026. The zoning appendix
identifies 2026-030 in its title/passage but uses 2025-079 on its second-page header.
Seek authoritative corrections and current instruments. All pages of both notices
were parsed and the material appendix pages visually inspected.

## Useful property-file requests

The [paid Property Inquiry Request](https://www.owensound.ca/business-building-development/property-inquiry-requests/)
requires a completed form and fee per property and estimates a minimum two to
three weeks. Choose the needed scopes: tax/water certificates, enforcement work
orders, current Building permit/order status, current planning/zoning, heritage
notice, agreement/completion/lot releases and securities, recent fire/building
reports, Fire field inspection and Engineering service availability. These are
separate scopes; current status is not a full historical property file. Service
availability is not an actual connection or capacity finding; camera investigation
is charged separately.

[Building guidance](https://www.owensound.ca/business-building-development/building-permits-inspections/)
allows occupancy permission for safe but incomplete work while the building
permit remains open until final inspection. Request both actual occupancy
permission and passed finals/outstanding conditions for each advertised unit.
An open permit alone does not establish unlawful occupancy. City guidance says
it does not conduct pre-purchase inspections.

The [seven-page undated ARU guide](https://media-001-ca.cdn.govstack.com/owensound-005-ca/media/tukbkfey/additional-residential-unit-guide.pdf)
describes one main-building and one accessory ARU, with separate converted
duplex/triplex and servicing guidance. Retain those as guide statements pending
the 2026 planning transition, current adopted unit rules, Building/ESA requirements,
actual servicing capacity and subject approvals. No legal-unit register or actual
unit permission was verified.

## Heritage, rentals and constraints

The [heritage page](https://www.owensound.ca/recreation-culture/heritage-conservation/)
distinguishes its online list and Part IV asterisk from the more detailed official
City Hall register. Its narrative count and older linked 2020 PPS are not current
verified inventories. PIR guidance warns that the register omits some potentially
significant properties. Interpretive plaques do not establish statutory designation.

The [STR registry](https://www.owensound.ca/business-building-development/short-term-rentals/str-registry/)
lists issued numbers and addresses, without verified expiry/current status. One
number occurs against different addresses: verify unique identity and current
licence, unit/occupancy, suspension and expiry. The table was not extracted or counted.

Original [GSCA regulation guidance](https://www.greysauble.on.ca/planning-regulations/plan-inputreview-regulations/)
uses Regulation 41/24 and requires activity/jurisdiction confirmation. Its
[mapping terms](https://www.greysauble.on.ca/mapping-gis/mapping-application-terms-of-use/)
restrict scraping, transfer and disclosure, with a separate attributed generated
map-product exception. No map agreement was accepted or property query performed.
County mapping warns of omitted wetlands/watercourses and says regulation text
prevails. Its separate SVCA notice's legacy 169/06 does not establish GSCA rules.
Historical County karst/ecological references and generalized provincial source
protection labels are separate from current parcel-wide original-authority evidence.
