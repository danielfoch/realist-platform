## App Review Information

**Notes for reviewer** (guideline 4.2 defense — paste into Review Notes):
> Realist is a native companion to realist.ca for Canadian real estate investors. Beyond the website, the app provides native capabilities: (1) push notifications for event announcements and deal alerts (APNs registration on first launch, tokens stored server-side), (2) account-backed event RSVP and ticketing used at real-world investor meetups, and (3) persistent saved deals, watchlists, and assumptions tied to the user's account. Core flows (deal analyzer, market data, leaderboard, meetups) work without login; creating a free account unlocks saving and alerts. No payment is required to use the app; event tickets are purchased for real-world services via Stripe checkout (physical-world goods, per guideline 3.1.5(a)).
