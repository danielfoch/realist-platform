On Replit the workspace and the live site are two separate things:

Workspace — where you just ran git reset and the psql migration. It's a dev environment.
Deployment — the published snapshot actually serving realist.ca.
Pulling code into the workspace doesn't touch the live site. Publishing takes a fresh snapshot of the workspace, builds it, and swaps it in. That's the step that's missing — which is why the bundle hash on realist.ca is still the old one.
